The Author

Yuvraj Shitole

Some stories are hidden within us and all around us. Shaping them, creating scenes, and giving them a narrative is what makes them beautiful. Observing the world and listening to the small, honest details is what breathes life into a story. I follow what my heart says and write what my mind narrates, so that each poem and book becomes a reflection of the world I see and the feelings I carry.

Y.S
